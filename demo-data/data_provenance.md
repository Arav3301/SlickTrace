# Vessel Track Data Provenance

## Source

- **Extracted from:** `SlickTrace_Demo_Bundle.zip` → `candidate_map.html`
- **Generated by:** Folium/Leaflet map produced from the real filtered historical AIS trajectories of the top 5 association candidates.
- **Investigation date:** 2018-09-26 (Sentinel-1A scene, acquisition ~00:01:45 UTC)

## Extraction Method

- Embedded Leaflet `L.polyline([lat, lon], ...)` coordinate arrays were extracted for the top 5 ranked vessels.
- Embedded Leaflet `L.marker([lat, lon], ...)` coordinates were extracted as the exact closest-approach points.
- Metadata was taken from the authoritative `candidate_ranking.csv` (full precision), cross-checked against the rounded display values in the Folium popup HTML.
- Every coordinate was converted from Leaflet order `[lat, lon]` to GeoJSON order `[lon, lat]`.
- **No interpolation, smoothing, or synthetic route generation was used.** All coordinates are verbatim values present in the source HTML.

## Caveats

- Tracks are the plotted segments shown in the source investigation map; they are filtered AIS points near the slick and are not necessarily the vessel's full voyage.
- Track point counts differ from the CSV `ais_points` column (which counts all filtered AIS points over the observation window); the GeoJSON reflects exactly what was plotted in `candidate_map.html`.
- Closest-approach coordinates are the vessel's closest-approach-to-slick positions as plotted in the source map.
